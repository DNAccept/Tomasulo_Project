# instructor_demo/

Evidence that your team ran and hand-verified the provided demo
**before** extending it — required by the Week 1 schedule.

## What goes here

- `demo_tomasulo_trace.log` — console output from running `demo_tomasulo.c`
  end-to-end (two independent ADD.D + dependent MUL.D).
- `hand_trace_verification.md` — your team's by-hand cycle-by-cycle trace of
  the same 4 instructions, compared line-by-line against the simulator's
  actual output. This is the artefact that proves you understand the
  mechanics before writing your own reservation-station logic.

## Why this matters

The Architecture Lead should be able to reproduce this hand trace live,
unaided, at any weekly checkpoint or the final defence.
