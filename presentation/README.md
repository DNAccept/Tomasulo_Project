# presentation/

## What goes here

- `paper_review_deck.pptx` — Week 1 paper-review presentation (ten-slide
  structure per Part I §6): citation, problem, motivation, method,
  diagrams, experimental setup, metrics, results, critical evaluation,
  connection to this project. 10–12 minutes + 5 min Q&A.
- `final_defence_deck.pptx` — Week 4 final defence deck, covering the
  completed implementation, experimental results, and innovation
  challenge.

Every team member must present a substantive component of the paper
review; the same expectation applies to the final defence.
