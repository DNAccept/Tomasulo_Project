# report/

## What goes here

`technical_report.pdf` — IEEE-style, 8–12 pages excluding appendices.

Must satisfy the Technical Report Checklist (Part V §14) at submission:

- IEEE formatting, 8–12 pages excluding appendices
- Every quantitative claim backed by a specific table/plot/figure
- Individual contribution statement, consistent with individual
  contribution forms
- AI-use declaration present and consistent with `ai_use_declaration/`
- Reproducibility statement with exact commands to regenerate every
  reported figure
- IEEE-style references, including full bibliographic detail for the
  paper-review sources
- Honest limitations/threats-to-validity section — at least one genuine
  limitation named
